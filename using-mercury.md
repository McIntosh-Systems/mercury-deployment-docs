# Using Mercury

## Conversations and deliverables

A person starts a conversation with a Bot and describes the work they need.
They can attach files, add instructions, and ask follow-up questions in the same
thread. A Bot can answer in chat or produce a deliverable such as a draft, report,
or spreadsheet. Files that a Bot saves to its workspace can be opened again from
Mercury.

## Work and follow-ups

Work gives a team a shared place to organize projects, assign tasks to people
and Bots, and follow progress. A task can record what needs to happen, who owns
it, and its current status. When scheduling is enabled, a person can also ask
their Bot for a reminder, a one-time follow-up, or recurring work.

## Computer use and oversight

With computer use enabled, a Bot can use a browser, work with files, or operate
supported applications. The person can follow the work in chat, view the Bot's
screen for supported computer sessions, and stop the run. Mercury presents an
approval when a supported action requires one. An Outlook send request, for
example, shows the message and its recipients so the person can review them
before sending.

## Preparing a meeting follow-up

With the relevant Microsoft 365 accounts connected, a person could ask:

> Find the latest Outlook thread about the delivery schedule, check when the
> next meeting is, read delivery-notes.txt in OneDrive, and draft a reply for me
> to review.

The Bot can collect the relevant details and prepare an Outlook draft. The
person can review or edit the draft before choosing to send it.
